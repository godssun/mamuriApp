```
At the end of the day, look back on today with Mamuri.

Mamuri is a diary app where you can record your daily emotions and receive warm, empathetic responses from an AI companion. When feelings are hard to handle alone, sharing them with Mamuri can make them a little lighter.

--- Your Own AI Friend ---
Create your very own AI companion in Mamuri.
- Give your friend a name
- Choose from 4 personality types: warm, calm, cheerful, or realistic
- Select formal or casual speech style
- Set a custom profile photo

--- Write a Diary, Get a Response ---
Write about your day and your feelings. Your AI friend will read your story and send back a message of empathy and comfort. No judgment, no unsolicited advice — just accepting your feelings as they are.

--- Continue the Conversation ---
Reply to your AI friend's response and keep the conversation going, just like chatting with a close friend.

--- Build a Writing Habit ---
See your diary entries at a glance on the calendar. Track your writing streak and build a consistent journaling habit.

--- Personalize Your Space ---
- Three background themes: warm, light, and dark
- Adjust text size and font to your preference
- Supports Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese

--- Safe and Transparent ---
- This app uses external AI services (LLM) to generate responses
- AI responses are for reference only and do not replace professional counseling or medical advice
- You can report inappropriate AI responses
```
